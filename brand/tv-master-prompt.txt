SUBJECT
A retro 1960s cathode-ray-tube television set, viewed strictly straight from
the front and left-right symmetrical (no perspective, no three-quarter view).
It has:
- a rounded-corner screen occupying the left two thirds of the body
- a narrow control panel on the right with exactly two round knobs
- two thin straight "rabbit ear" antennas rising in a V from the top
- two short splayed legs at the bottom

STYLE
Flat vector illustration. Clean geometric shapes, thick confident forms,
minimal detail, no texture, no photorealism, no drop shadows, no gradients on
the cabinet. The icon must stay readable when scaled down to 16x16 pixels, so:
strong silhouette, nothing thinner than about 3% of the image width, no text,
no small ornaments, no fine grille lines.

COLORS - use these exactly
- television cabinet: violet, #7c5cff for the lit faces and #4a3aa8 for the
  shaded faces
- screen: bright cyan #22d3ee, with a slightly lighter soft glow in the middle
- knobs and the two antenna tips: warm amber #ffc857
- outlines and inner shadow: near black #0a0c11
Background: fully transparent (alpha), not white.

COMPOSITION
Square canvas 1024x1024. The television including its antennas fills about 86%
of the canvas, optically centred, with even margins on all sides.
