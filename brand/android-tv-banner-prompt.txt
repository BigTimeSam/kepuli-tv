The Android TV app's launcher banner (brand/android-tv-banner-1280x720.png),
which the app carries as its banner (android/app/build.gradle) and Google Play
asks for as the TV banner. Made by giving Codex the prompt below in an empty
directory holding brand/tv-master-1024.png as logo.png:

  codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -s workspace-write -C <dir> "<prompt>" </dev/null

then cropped to 16:9 and scaled:

  sips -c 940 1672 tv-banner.png && sips -z 720 1280 tv-banner.png --out brand/android-tv-banner-1280x720.png

The name is drawn by the image model: read it letter by letter in what comes
back before using it.

==================================================================== PROMPT

Use your image generation tool to create ONE image and save it as the PNG file
"tv-banner.png" in your current working directory. Do not write any code to
draw it; use the image generation tool directly. When the file is saved, print
its absolute path and stop.

The file "logo.png" in this directory is the product's logo. Look at it first
and reproduce that television faithfully: it is the reference for the shape,
the colours and the outline weight.

PURPOSE
The launcher banner of an Android TV app called Kepuli-TV: the wide tile a
television's home screen shows for the app, read from across a room and shown
quite small (320 by 180), so it must be bold and simple.

SUBJECT
- On the left third: the logo's retro 1960s cathode-ray-tube television, seen
  straight from the front: a violet cabinet, a rounded-corner bright cyan
  screen on the left two thirds of it with a soft lighter glow in the middle,
  a narrow control panel on the right with exactly two round amber knobs, two
  thin "rabbit ear" antennas in a V with amber tips, two short splayed legs.
- To the right of it, vertically centred with the television: the name
  "Kepuli-TV" on one line, spelled exactly K-e-p-u-l-i, a hyphen, T-V, in a
  clean geometric sans-serif typeface, semibold, off-white #e7eaf2, large:
  the capital letters about a third of the canvas height.
- Nothing else: no other words, no tagline, no icons, no borders, no frame,
  no rounded corners on the canvas.

STYLE
Flat vector illustration. Clean geometric shapes, thick confident forms, bold
dark outlines of even weight on the television, minimal detail, no texture,
no photorealism, no 3D, no drop shadows.

COLOURS - use only these
- violet #7c5cff (lit faces) and #4a3aa8 (shaded faces) for the cabinet
- bright cyan #22d3ee for the screen
- warm amber #ffc857 for the knobs and the antenna tips
- near black #0a0c11 for the outlines
- background: near black #0a0c11 filling the whole canvas edge to edge, with
  one soft, wide violet glow (#7c5cff at low strength) behind the television
  fading smoothly into the near black. No pattern, no stars, no grid.
- the name in off-white #e7eaf2

COMPOSITION
Wide landscape canvas, 16:9. The television and the name together are
centred as one group and fill about 80% of the canvas width, with clear empty
margin on every side so the image can be cropped a little to exactly 16:9.
