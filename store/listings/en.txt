=== NAME ===
Kepuli-TV – IPTV Player
=== SHORT DESCRIPTION ===
Watch your own Xtream Codes IPTV in your browser without fighting the app. Channels open in seconds, and MKV plays with subtitles.
=== DETAILED DESCRIPTION ===
Watch your own IPTV subscription in your browser without fighting the app.
Kepuli-TV is an Xtream Codes player built around one idea: a huge catalogue
should feel small.

→ Channels open in a second or two, even from an enormous catalogue
→ Hundreds of messy categories become a tidy list of countries
→ A full-window programme guide (EPG) with catch-up where offered
→ MKV movies and episodes play right in the browser, subtitles and surround
  sound included


WHY I MADE IT

I got tired of IPTV apps that made me fight them: endless category lists,
dialogs that froze everything while a catalogue loaded, and files that simply
would not play. So I built the one I wanted to use every evening.
- Samuli, developer of Kepuli-TV


WHAT YOU GET

• Paste the M3U link your provider sent you and the settings fill themselves.
• Search that ignores accents and word order, and a filter in every menu.
• Whatever you left halfway waits at the top, and the next episode starts by
  itself.
• Nothing blocks the screen while data loads, and what is playing keeps
  playing.
• Made for accounts with one connection: switching channels waits for the
  provider to free it instead of failing.
• Movies and series as covers, with plots, ratings and seasons.
• Favourites, viewing history and resume positions.
• Hide the global brands from a country's list, and its own stations are
  easy to find.
• Cast to a Chromecast, use the media keys, and get a reminder before a
  programme starts.
• A dark or a light look, and the keyboard reaches everything; the layout
  follows your text size and contrast settings.
• In 30 languages. It speaks your browser's language until you choose
  another.


PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No tracking, no ads, no sign-up. Kepuli-TV talks only to your own server and
asks permission for that address alone — wider access only if you allow it,
when your provider sends streams elsewhere.


PLEASE NOTE

The extension contains no content and comes with no provider. Bring an Xtream
Codes account of your own, use it only for content you have the right to
watch, and make sure the service is lawful where you live.


GET STARTED

Click the toolbar icon, enter your server, username and password, and your
list appears.
