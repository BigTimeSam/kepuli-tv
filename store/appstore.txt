=== DESCRIPTION en-GB ===
Kepuli-TV plays the IPTV subscription you already have: an M3U playlist, an Xtream Codes account or a Stalker portal. It is built around one idea: a huge catalogue should feel small.

• Channels open in a second or two, even from an enormous catalogue
• Hundreds of messy categories become a tidy list of countries
• A programme guide (EPG) that fills the screen, with catch-up where your provider offers it
• MKV films and episodes play, with their subtitles
• Cast films and channels to a Chromecast, with the iPhone or the iPad as its remote control
• A reminder before a programme starts, as a notification


WHAT YOU GET

• Paste the link your provider sent you and the settings fill themselves.
• Several accounts side by side: Xtream Codes, an M3U playlist with its XMLTV guide, or a Stalker portal with your box's MAC address. A playlist can also be a file from the Files app.
• Search that ignores accents and word order, and a filter in every menu.
• Whatever you left halfway waits at the top, and the next episode starts by itself.
• Nothing blocks the screen while data loads, and what is playing keeps playing, even when the connection drops.
• Made for accounts with one connection: switching channels waits for the provider to free it instead of failing.
• Films and series as covers, with plots, ratings and seasons.
• Favourites, viewing history and resume positions.
• Hide the global brands from a country's list, and its own stations are easy to find.
• Keep only the countries you watch, and only the film and series categories you want.
• The guide can show just your favourite channels, and you can search the programmes of every channel at once.
• Filter films by year, rating and length.
• Each film once in the list, its language versions on its own page.
• Upright or on its side, on the iPhone and the iPad, in a dark or a light look.
• In 30 languages. It speaks your device's language until you choose another.


CASTING

Choose Cast to a Chromecast and pick the device. The Chromecast fetches the stream itself and the app becomes its remote control. Films in MP4 and channels sent as HLS can be cast when your provider's server allows it; anything else plays on your device, and the app tells you.


PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your accounts, favourites and history stay on your device. Kepuli-TV talks to your own provider's server, and channel logos may load from a public logo collection. Casting uses Google's Cast software, which sends Google the diagnostics its privacy label names.


PLEASE NOTE

Kepuli-TV contains no channels, films or playlists and comes with no provider. You need a subscription of your own. Use it only for content you have the right to watch, and make sure the service is lawful where you live.


GET STARTED

Open the app and paste the address your provider sent you, or enter your server, user name and password, or a portal with its MAC address. Your channels appear.

--- EXPORT COMPLIANCE -------------------------------------------------------

Info.plist carries ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false, so the question is
not asked at each upload. The reasoning, should it be asked:

  • Network encryption is the operating system's own TLS (URLSession and
    WebKit). The app brings no TLS of its own.
  • hls.js decrypts AES-128 HLS segments when a provider's playlist names a
    key. That is decryption of media for playback with a standard algorithm
    and a key the stream supplies; the app encrypts nothing with it.
  • The provider code computes hashes (the checks a portal's sign-in asks
    for, and names for the cache). A hash is not encryption.
  • The Cast SDK talks to the device over the system's TLS.
  • No user data is encrypted by the app, and nothing is offered for
    encrypting files, messages or storage.

All of it is exempt: authentication, standard encryption within the
operating system, and media playback. No annual self-classification report
is filed.

=== REVIEW NOTES ===
WHAT THE APP IS
Kepuli-TV is a video player for an IPTV subscription the viewer already has. It contains no streams, films, playlists, provider addresses, provider list or accounts, and it plays nothing until the viewer enters their own provider's details. The one list it carries is an index of file names from a public collection of channel logos, used only to show a logo beside a channel that the viewer's own provider already lists; nothing can be found or played with it. It does not save or download streams: there is no such function in the app.

DEMO ACCOUNT (invented content only, made for this review)
1. Open the app. "Connect to your provider" appears.
2. Under Connection choose "Server and credentials (Xtream Codes)".
3. Protocol: HTTPS. Server: <DEMO HOST>. Port: 443. Username: demo. Password: demo.
4. Tap Connect. Choose a country, then a channel: it plays.
5. Movies and Series hold demo films; Series > Nordic Noir > Silent Fjord is an MKV file with subtitles. The programme guide is the button above the channel list.
Every title, channel and programme in the demo is fictional and belongs to the project.

NATIVE FEATURES
• Google Cast sender: Cast to a Chromecast in the player's menu opens Google's device dialog; the Chromecast then plays the stream and the app is its remote control. The very first time, Google's note on finding devices and the system's local-network question come first, and choosing Cast again opens the device list.
• Local notifications: Remind me on a programme in the guide schedules a notification a minute before it starts (Settings > Show reminders as notifications).
• Files: a playlist can be picked from the Files app.
• The screen stays awake while a picture plays, links open in a Safari view over the player, and Open in VLC hands a stream to VLC.
• Full screen: the button in the picture's corner or a double tap gives the picture the whole screen; the same, or a swipe down, leaves it.
• Portrait and landscape on the iPhone and the iPad, Split View on the iPad, 30 languages.
What sets it apart from other players: channels grouped by country out of a provider's hundreds of categories, a full programme guide with search across channels, MKV playback with subtitle tracks, and accounts of three kinds side by side.

CHROMECAST CANNOT BE TRIED WITHOUT A DEVICE
Without a Chromecast on the network, Google's dialog opens and finds nothing, and the app goes on playing on the device. The attached video shows a session with a real device: choosing it, a film playing on the television, the remote control, and stopping.

PERMISSIONS AND KEYS
• Local network: asked when the viewer first casts (to find Chromecast devices), or earlier when the viewer's own provider is at an address on their home network.
• Notifications: asked when the viewer sets a first reminder, or turns Show reminders as notifications on in Settings.
• NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsInWebContent: the viewer types the address of their own provider, and many providers serve plain http. Settings warns that an http connection is unencrypted and recommends https. No other transport exception is set.
• Background mode "audio": so that sound continues, and picture in picture stays up, when the viewer leaves the app during playback.

CONTENT AND RIGHTS
The viewer supplies the subscription and is responsible for their right to watch it; the description says so, and the first screen says that the app comes with no channels of its own. The developer has no relationship with any provider.
