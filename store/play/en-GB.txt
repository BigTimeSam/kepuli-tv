=== NAME ===
Kepuli-TV – IPTV Player
=== SHORT DESCRIPTION ===
Play your own IPTV: Xtream Codes, M3U or Stalker. Fast channels, guide, MKV.
=== DETAILED DESCRIPTION ===
Watch your own IPTV subscription on your phone, tablet or television without
fighting the app. Kepuli-TV plays Xtream Codes accounts, M3U playlists and
Stalker portals, built around one idea: a huge catalogue should feel small.

→ Channels open in a second or two, even from an enormous catalogue
→ Hundreds of messy categories become a tidy list of countries
→ A full-screen programme guide (EPG) with catch-up where offered
→ MKV movies and episodes play, subtitles and surround sound included
→ On a television everything works with the remote control
→ On a phone the picture stays in a small window when you go elsewhere, and
  the sound goes on with the screen off


WHAT YOU GET

• Paste the M3U link your provider sent you and the settings fill themselves.
• Several accounts: Xtream Codes, an M3U playlist with its XMLTV guide, or a
  Stalker portal with your box's MAC address. Switch in one tap.
• Search that ignores accents and word order, and a filter in every menu.
• Whatever you left halfway waits at the top, and the next episode starts by
  itself.
• Nothing blocks the screen while data loads, and what is playing keeps
  playing, even when the connection drops.
• Made for accounts with one connection: switching channels waits for the
  provider to free it instead of failing.
• Movies and series as covers, with plots, ratings and seasons.
• Favourites, viewing history and resume positions.
• Hide the global brands from a country's list, and its own stations are
  easy to find.
• Keep only the countries you watch, and only the film and series
  categories you want.
• A reminder before a programme starts, as a notification, even when the
  app is closed.
• The guide can show just your favourite channels.
• Search the programmes of every channel at once.
• Filter movies by year, rating and length, or show only your favourites.
• Each film once in the list, its language versions on its own page.
• A dark or a light look, upright or lying down.
• In 30 languages.


PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No tracking, no ads, no sign-up. Kepuli-TV talks only to your own provider's
server. The one exception: channel logos may load from a public logo
collection on GitHub.


PLEASE NOTE

The app contains no content and comes with no provider. Bring your own
account, use it only for content you have the right to watch, and make sure
the service is lawful where you live.


GET STARTED

Open the app and enter your server and credentials, a playlist link, or a
portal with its MAC address, and your list appears.
