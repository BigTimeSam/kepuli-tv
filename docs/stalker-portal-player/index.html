Stalker portal player in the browser

A provider that sells its service for MAG set-top boxes gives a portal address, often ending in /c/, and registers a MAC address for the box. There is no user name or password. Kepuli-TV talks to such a portal from a browser tab, so the subscription can be watched without the box.

On Android, Kepuli-TV installs in Firefox: how to set it up.

On an iPhone, Kepuli-TV installs in the Orion browser: how to set it up.

Connect to a portal

  1. Install Kepuli-TV and click its toolbar icon.
  2. Paste the portal's address into Subscription or playlist address. An address ending in /c/ is recognised, and the form moves to Stalker portal (MAC address).
  3. Enter the MAC address registered to your subscription.
  4. Press Connect and choose Allow when the browser asks whether Kepuli-TV may reach the portal.

Use only the MAC address registered to your own subscription. Another person's, or one from a shared list, may not be used: see the terms of use.

What works

  • Channels, films and series as the portal lists them.
  • The portal's own programme data in the list and in the programme guide.
  • Reconnection: a portal's stream link is often good for one connection only, so the player asks for a new one whenever a live stream is opened again.

What does not, yet

  • The portal's archive (catch-up) is not there yet.
  • Sync leaves a Stalker account out unless Include passwords is on, as the MAC address is as good as a password.

How it reaches the portal

A portal expects a MAG box: the MAC address in a cookie and a set-top box's name as the browser's. A web page cannot set those itself, so the extension sets them for its own requests to the portal's server alone, and the rule is gone when the browser closes. The details are in the user guide.

Get Kepuli-TV

Free and open source. It comes with no channels: bring your own subscription, and use it only for content you have the right to watch.
