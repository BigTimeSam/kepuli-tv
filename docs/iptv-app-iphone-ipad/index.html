Kepuli-TV on the iPhone and the iPad

The Kepuli-TV app plays your own IPTV subscription on an iPhone or an iPad: live channels with a programme guide, films and series. It comes with no channels and no provider. You bring the M3U playlist, the Xtream Codes account or the Stalker portal you already have.

Connect your subscription

  1. Open Kepuli-TV. The first time, it asks how to reach your provider.
  2. Paste the address your provider sent you. A link that carries your user name and password fills the other fields by itself.
  3. Press Connect. Your channels, films and series appear.

The three kinds of subscription

  • Xtream Codes: the server's address, your user name and your password.
  • M3U playlist: the playlist's address, or a playlist file picked from the Files app with Choose a file…. Its programme guide comes from the XMLTV address the playlist names, or from one you enter.
  • Stalker portal: the portal's address and the MAC address your provider registered for your box.

Cast to a Chromecast

  • Choose Cast to a Chromecast on the top bar and pick the device. The first time, the system asks whether Kepuli-TV may find devices on your local network: allow it. Before that, Google shows a note of its own. After both, the devices are listed as soon as one is found. If none is found within half a minute, choose Cast to a Chromecast once more. Once a Chromecast has been found, the Cast mark is also at the top of the screen.
  • The Chromecast fetches the stream by itself, and the iPhone or the iPad is its remote control: Remote control opens the controls, and Stop casting ends the session, after which the picture plays on here.
  • Films in MP4 and channels sent as HLS can be cast, a channel only when the provider's server lets another device read it. Anything else, an MKV film for one, plays here, and Kepuli-TV says so.

Reminders

  • In the programme guide, open a programme that has not started yet and choose Remind me.
  • With Show reminders as notifications turned on in Settings, the reminder arrives as a notification a minute before the programme starts, also when Kepuli-TV is closed. Tap it and the channel starts.

How the app behaves

  • It follows the way you hold the device, upright or on its side, in full screen as well.
  • The screen stays on while a picture plays.
  • The sound goes on when you leave the app or lock the screen while something plays. The lock screen and Control Centre pause and resume it. Picture in picture in the ☰ menu keeps the picture in sight over other apps.
  • Open in VLC in the ☰ menu hands the stream to the VLC app. Without VLC, the App Store opens at its page.
  • Links to a film's IMDb page, a trailer or this guide open over the player and close back to it.
  • Everything stays on the device: your accounts, favourites, history and settings. The app has no recording.

Good to know

  • Kepuli-TV itself collects nothing. What the Chromecast part of the app sends to Google is in the privacy policy.
  • The licences of the code the app is built on are on the licences page.
  • Questions and faults go to the project's issues on GitHub.
