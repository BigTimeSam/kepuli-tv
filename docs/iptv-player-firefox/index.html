IPTV player for Firefox

Kepuli-TV is an add-on for Mozilla Firefox 130 or newer that plays your own IPTV subscription in a tab: live channels with a programme guide, films and series. The same add-on runs in Firefox for Android and, through Orion, on the iPhone. It is free and open source, and it comes with no channels of its own.

On an iPhone, Kepuli-TV installs in the Orion browser: how to set it up.

Install it in Firefox

  1. Open Kepuli-TV on Firefox Add-ons with the button above and choose Add to Firefox.
  2. Click the Kepuli-TV icon in the toolbar. If it is not there, it is under the puzzle piece, where it can be pinned to the toolbar.
  3. The player opens in a tab of its own and asks for your provider's details.

Connect your subscription

Paste the address your provider sent you. The form works out which kind it is: an M3U playlist, an Xtream Codes account or a Stalker portal. Firefox then asks whether Kepuli-TV may reach that server: choose Allow, which covers that server only. Nothing is sent anywhere before that.

How it plays in Firefox

  • MKV files are unpacked in the tab and play with seeking. Firefox has no decoder for AC-3, E-AC-3 or DTS sound, so the player brings its own: see MKV in the browser.
  • Logos and covers that a provider serves over plain http are shown in Firefox too.
  • Sync between computers goes through Firefox's own sync, and is off until you turn it on.
  • Chromecast is not offered: Firefox has no way to reach one. The player's menu hands the stream to VLC or another player instead.

Good to know

  • The programme guide, catch-up, recording and the thirty languages are the same as in Chrome.
  • Everything else is in the user guide.

Get Kepuli-TV

Free and open source. It comes with no channels: bring your own subscription, and use it only for content you have the right to watch.
