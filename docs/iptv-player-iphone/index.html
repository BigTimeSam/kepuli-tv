IPTV player for the iPhone, in Orion

Safari and the other browsers on iOS do not install browser extensions of this kind. Orion, a browser by Kagi, does: it installs Firefox add-ons on the iPhone, and Kepuli-TV is one of them. Your own M3U playlist, Xtream Codes account or Stalker portal then plays in an Orion tab.

Add to Firefox

Install it on the iPhone

  1. Install Orion Browser by Kagi from the App Store.
  2. In Orion's menu choose Extensions, then + and Install Firefox extension, search for Kepuli-TV and install it.
  3. Choose Kepuli-TV in Orion's menu. The player opens in a tab.
  4. Paste the address your provider sent you and press Connect.

How it plays on the iPhone

  • Live channels play through the iPhone's own player as HLS. A channel may wait for a tap on Play before it starts, as iOS allows sound only after a touch.
  • Films and episodes play too, an MKV file read a piece at a time.
  • Full screen is the iPhone's own video player, with subtitles, and AirPlay is among its controls.
  • Open in VLC in the player's menu hands the stream to the VLC app. Without VLC, the App Store opens at its page.
  • Chromecast is not offered: no browser on the iPhone reaches one.
  • A planned recording waits for the player to be open in a tab.

Good to know

  • Kepuli-TV has been measured on the iPhone. Orion also runs on the iPad, where it has not been tried yet.
  • On Android the route is Firefox: see Android.
  • Everything else is in the user guide.

Get Kepuli-TV

Free and open source. It comes with no channels: bring your own subscription, and use it only for content you have the right to watch.
