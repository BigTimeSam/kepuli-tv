# Xtream Codes player in the browser

If your provider gave you a server address, a user name and a password, you
have an Xtream Codes account. Kepuli-TV signs in to it from a browser tab
and shows what the server has: live channels by category, films, series by
season, the programme guide and the channels' archive.

## Connect an Xtream Codes account

1. Install Kepuli-TV and click its toolbar icon.
2. The quick way: paste the whole address your provider sent
   (`get.php?username=…&password=…`) into **Subscription or playlist
   address**. The server, port, user name and password are filled in under
   **Server and credentials (Xtream Codes)**.
3. Or choose **Server and credentials (Xtream Codes)** yourself and type
   them in. The eye at the end of the password field shows what was typed.
4. Press **Connect**. The browser asks whether Kepuli-TV may reach that
   server: choose **Allow**, which covers that server only.

## What you get from the server

- **Live channels** by country and topic, with the programme on now and a
  progress bar on every row.
- **Films** with plot, rating, director, cast and the file's format, and
  **series** by season with their covers.
- **The programme guide** and **catch-up** for channels with an archive:
  see [the programme guide](/iptv-epg-guide/).
- **Your account's details** under **Settings › Account & data**, the
  number of streams it allows at once among them.
- **Resume points**: a film or an episode goes on from where you stopped,
  and the next episode follows.

## If the connection fails

The message under **Connection failed** says whose side the fault is likely
on and what to try: the address and the port, the user name and password
(copied rather than typed), the provider's server being down, or the
account itself having run out. When every connection of the account is in
use, it says that instead.

## Good to know

- Several accounts can be kept side by side and switched between, each with
  its own favourites and history.
- Your credentials stay in the browser's own storage on your device. See
  the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
- Kepuli-TV is only a player: it sells and recommends no subscriptions.
- A portal with a MAC address instead of a password is a
  [Stalker portal](/stalker-portal-player/).
