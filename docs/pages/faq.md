# Questions and answers

Short answers about Kepuli-TV, the free IPTV player extension for Chrome
and Firefox, with a link to the page that has the steps.

## Is Kepuli-TV free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT licence. There is no account
with us, no paid version, and the extension sends us nothing.

## Does Kepuli-TV come with channels?

No. It is only a player. It comes with no channels, playlists or media and
plays nothing until you add an account or a playlist from your own
provider. We do not sell, provide or recommend IPTV subscriptions.

## Which browsers does it run in?

Google Chrome 116 or newer and Mozilla Firefox 130 or newer on a computer,
Firefox for Android 142 or newer, and Orion on the iPhone. See
[Chrome](/iptv-player-chrome/), [Firefox](/iptv-player-firefox/),
[Android](/iptv-player-android/) and [iPhone](/iptv-player-iphone/).

## Is Kepuli-TV affiliated with any IPTV provider?

No, and not with Google, Mozilla or Kagi either. Use it only for content
you have the right to watch.

## Does it work on Android?

Yes, in Firefox for Android. Chrome for Android does not install
extensions. The steps are on the [Android page](/iptv-player-android/).

## Does it work on the iPhone?

Yes, in the Orion browser, which installs Firefox add-ons on iOS. Safari
does not. The steps are on the [iPhone page](/iptv-player-iphone/).

## How do I add an M3U playlist?

Paste its address into the first field, or choose the file, and press
**Connect**. See [M3U playlists](/m3u-playlist-player/).

## How do I connect with Xtream Codes?

Paste the address your provider sent, or enter the server, user name and
password. See [Xtream Codes](/xtream-codes-player/).

## Does it support Stalker portals and MAC addresses?

Yes: the portal's address and the MAC address registered to your own
subscription. See [Stalker portals](/stalker-portal-player/).

## Is there a programme guide (EPG)?

Yes, with catch-up, programme search and reminders. See
[the programme guide](/iptv-epg-guide/).

## Can I watch films and series?

Yes. Films and series have tabs of their own, with covers, plots and
resume points, and MKV files play in the tab. See
[MKV in the browser](/mkv-player-browser/).

## Do I need a separate media player?

No. Everything plays in the browser tab. For the rare file the browser
cannot play, the player's menu hands the stream to VLC.

## Can I record?

Yes: the channel you watch, or a programme planned from the guide.
Recording is off until you turn it on in the settings, and recordings are
for your own use.

## Where are my user name and password kept?

In the browser's own storage on your device. They reach your other
computers only if you turn on sync and include passwords. See the
[privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Which languages does it speak?

Thirty, switched in the settings.

## Something does not work. Where do I ask?

The [user guide](https://github.com/BigTimeSam/kepuli-tv/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#if-something-goes-wrong) covers the usual causes. For
a bug, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/BigTimeSam/kepuli-tv/issues)
with your browser and the steps, and leave out your credentials.
