# IPTV player for Chrome

Kepuli-TV is an extension for Google Chrome 116 or newer that plays your own
IPTV subscription in a tab: live channels with a programme guide, films and
series. It is free and open source, there is no account with us, and it
comes with no channels of its own.

## Install it in Chrome

1. Open Kepuli-TV in the Chrome Web Store with the button above and choose
   **Add to Chrome**.
2. Click the puzzle piece in Chrome's toolbar and pin Kepuli-TV, so that its
   icon stays in sight.
3. Click the icon. The player opens in a tab of its own and asks for your
   provider's details.

## Connect your subscription

Paste the address your provider sent you. The form works out which kind it
is: an [M3U playlist](/m3u-playlist-player/), an
[Xtream Codes account](/xtream-codes-player/) or a
[Stalker portal](/stalker-portal-player/). Chrome then asks whether
Kepuli-TV may reach that server: choose **Allow**, which covers that server
only.

## What Chrome adds

- **Chromecast**: a file Chrome plays by itself goes to the device from the
  player's menu, and for the rest the menu explains how Chrome's own tab
  casting does it.
- **Picture in picture** from the same menu, for a channel that stays in a
  corner of the screen while you work.
- **Two channels side by side**, when your subscription allows two streams
  at once or you have a second account.
- **Sync between computers** through Chrome's own sync, off until you turn
  it on.

## Good to know

- Chrome on Android and on the iPhone does not install extensions. On a
  phone, see [Android](/iptv-player-android/) and
  [iPhone](/iptv-player-iphone/).
- MKV files play in the tab, seeking included, with AC-3, E-AC-3 and DTS
  sound: see [MKV in the browser](/mkv-player-browser/).
- Everything else is in the [user guide](https://github.com/BigTimeSam/kepuli-tv/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md).
