# IPTV player with a programme guide

Kepuli-TV reads the programme data (EPG) of your subscription and shows it
where you choose what to watch: on every channel's row, under the picture,
and as a full guide with time across and channels down.

## Where the programme data comes from

- **An Xtream Codes account** brings its own. See
  [Xtream Codes](/xtream-codes-player/).
- **An M3U playlist** names an XMLTV guide in its header, or you enter one
  under **Programme guide address (XMLTV)**. See
  [M3U playlists](/m3u-playlist-player/).
- **A Stalker portal** brings its own as well.

## The guide

**Programme guide** above the channel list, or the `g` key, turns the
window into a grid: channels as rows, time on the horizontal axis and a
moving line at now. The channel goes on playing in the corner.

- The grid shows the channels of the list, so a country, a topic or a
  search narrows the guide too, and the star shows your favourites alone.
- It runs five days forward, and back as far as the channels' archive
  reaches.
- A click on a programme on now plays the channel. One that has ended or is
  still to come shows its description and what can be done with it.

## Catch-up

A channel with an archive gets **Watch catch-up** on its past programmes,
and **Start from the beginning** on the one on now. This is the provider's
archive, so how far back it goes depends on the service.

## Reminders, search and recording

- **Remind me** on an upcoming programme: a minute before it starts, a
  message names the programme and its channel, as a notification of the
  system's too, if you allow it.
- **Search programmes** looks through channel names, titles and
  descriptions, among past programmes, upcoming ones or both.
- **Record** plans a recording of a programme from the guide. Recording is
  off until you turn it on in the settings.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Record only programmes you have the right to keep, for your own use.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen the guide gives nearly everything to the grid: swipe to
move in time, and a tap on a programme opens its details as a sheet from
the bottom. See [Android](/iptv-player-android/) and
[iPhone](/iptv-player-iphone/).

The whole of it is in the [user guide](https://github.com/BigTimeSam/kepuli-tv/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md#the-programme-guide).
