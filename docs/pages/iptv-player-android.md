# IPTV player for Android, in Firefox

On an Android phone or tablet Kepuli-TV runs in Firefox, which installs
add-ons there as it does on a computer. Chrome for Android does not install
extensions, so Firefox it is. You get the same player: live channels with a
programme guide, films and series from your own M3U playlist, Xtream Codes
account or Stalker portal.

## Install it on Android

1. Install [Firefox](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.mozilla.firefox)
   (142 or newer) from the Play Store.
2. Open this page in Firefox and choose **Add to Firefox** above, or choose
   **Extensions** in Firefox's menu and search for Kepuli-TV there.
3. Open Firefox's menu, choose **Extensions** and then **Kepuli-TV**. The
   player opens in a tab.
4. Paste the address your provider sent you and press **Connect**. Firefox
   asks whether Kepuli-TV may reach that server: choose **Allow**.

## What differs on a phone

- **The lists and the guide fit the screen.** The filters fold away, and
  the [programme guide](/iptv-epg-guide/) gives nearly the whole screen to
  its grid: swipe to move in time, tap a programme for its details.
- **Full screen** is a button in the header, and takes the player out of
  the system's bars as well.
- **Open in VLC** in the player's menu hands the stream to the VLC app,
  which can send it on to a Chromecast. Without VLC, the Play Store opens
  at its page.
- **MKV films play with sound**, AC-3, E-AC-3 and DTS included: see
  [MKV in the browser](/mkv-player-browser/).
- **Two channels side by side** are for a tablet; on a phone's screen the
  choice is not offered.
- **The screen stays on** while a picture plays.

## Good to know

- An Android TV or a set-top box has not been tried: this is for phones and
  tablets with Firefox.
- On an iPhone the route is another browser: see
  [iPhone](/iptv-player-iphone/).
- Everything else is in the [user guide](https://github.com/BigTimeSam/kepuli-tv/blob/main/docs/user-guide.md).
