    Accounts

      Your provider’s address and credentials. Where to find these details

      Connection
      Paste the address your provider gave you. If it has a username and password in it, they fill in the connection fields; any other M3U playlist address is used as it is.

      Use HTTPS when your provider supports it. With HTTP, your username, password and viewing requests travel without encryption and can be read or changed by others on the network.

      Subscription

      Not connected yet. The account’s details appear here once the connection is made.

      Sync between computers

      Carried by your browser’s own sync to the computers signed in to the same browser account. Nothing goes to Kepuli-TV.

      Manage stored data

      Categories, lists, covers and programme data are fetched again. Favourites and history stay.
      Credentials, settings, favourites, history and the cache all go. This cannot be undone.

      Next, your browser asks to let Kepuli-TV reach this server. Choose Allow: it is needed for the lists and the streams, and covers this server only.
